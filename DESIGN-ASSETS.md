## Image generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a website demonstration asset: a precisely aligned side-by-side before/after of the SAME contemporary living room, two equal-width panels, no gap or border. Overall landscape 3:2 image, each half is a portrait crop of the exact same camera view. LEFT HALF: empty room, warm white walls, oak plank floor, large black-framed windows at the left, afternoon sun, rear wall blank. RIGHT HALF: identical architecture, camera angle, floor, windows and lighting, professionally virtually staged with an oatmeal linen sofa against rear wall, walnut low coffee table, ivory textured rug, olive tree and warm minimal art. Calm editorial architectural photography, highly realistic materials, tasteful understated design. Strictly identical room geometry in both halves for an interactive before-after slider that overlays the two halves. No words, no labels, no people, no logos. This is an illustrative demonstration of virtual staging, not a real property or customer result.

- `dist/media/sad-man-sketch.webp`
- `dist/media/worried-woman-sketch.webp`
- `dist/media/angry-man-sketch.webp`

Shared prompt: “Use case: illustration-story. Asset: small website editorial pencil portrait icon. A single centered head and a hint of neck only, drawn with dark graphite pencil, loose confident hand-drawn contours, subtle crosshatching and beautiful irregular strokes. Simple enough to read as an 80px icon. Restrained expressive editorial sketch, human and approachable, not emoji, not photorealistic. Front three-quarter view, entire head and hair visible, generous transparent margin. Dark graphite on genuinely transparent background. No paper rectangle, no circle, no border, no words, no numbers, no watermarks. Match a premium warm cream and sage educational website. Square composition.”

Subject variants: sad adult man with downcast eyes and short wavy hair; worried adult woman with raised inner eyebrows and shoulder-length hair; frustrated angry adult man with knitted brows and short textured hair, no screaming.
